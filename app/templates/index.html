The Nashik Legacy

A Symphony of Spices
Since 1995

"Food is not just nourishment. It is memory, tradition, and devotion served with warmth."

Founded in Nashik by Ramchandra Bhagshree, Hotel Bhagshree began with a sacred promise — to cook with the same purity, care, and devotion that you would for your own family. From our humble beginnings near the sacred Godavari ghats to our renowned restaurant on College Road, we have become Nashik's cherished home for authentic regional dining.

We source cold-pressed oils, pure cow ghee, stone-ground local masalas, and crisp morning harvest from Godavari valley farmers every sunrise. Every dish brings the authentic richness of Maharashtra and beyond.
