The Beginning

A Kitchen Born from Devotion & Taste

"I wanted to offer people the pure, comforting taste of homemade food served with boundless affection."
— Ramchandra Bhagshree, Founder

In 1995, amidst the spiritual serenity of the holy city of Nashik, Ramchandra Bhagshree started Hotel Bhagshree on College Road. With a modest stove, his mother’s heirloom stone-ground masala blends, and milk sourced fresh from local Trimbak dairies, he set out to serve the most wholesome food in the region.

The word of our authentic Nashik Misal, soothing Varan Bhat with pure ghee, and slow-braised Chicken Kolhapuri spread across Khandesh and beyond. Over three decades, Hotel Bhagshree has become an essential stop for pilgrims, vineyard visitors, and local families celebrating their happiest occasions.

Milestones

Three Decades of Serving Nashik

The journey of building a culinary landmark in the grape capital of India.

1995
The First Table
Opened on College Road, Nashik serving authentic spicy Misal Pav and hand-churned Taak (spiced buttermilk).
2003
Family Dining Hall
Expanded to a spacious 60-seat hall to welcome families and travelers along the Mumbai-Nashik highway.
2014
Seven Cuisines
Introduced tandoor ovens and South Indian griddles, establishing our acclaimed multi-cuisine kitchen.
Today
Pride of Nashik
Serving over 500,000 satisfied guests with pure quality, farm-to-table ingredients, and love.
